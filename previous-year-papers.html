Chemical Reactions and Equations

Open this chapter →
A magnesium ribbon is burned in air. Write the balanced chemical equation for the reaction and identify its type. 3 marks ⌄
2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. This is a combination reaction — two reactants (magnesium and oxygen) combine to form a single product (magnesium oxide). The reaction is also an oxidation of magnesium, visible as a bright white flame and a white ash residue.
Why does the colour of copper sulphate solution change when an iron nail is dipped into it? Name the type of reaction. 2 marks ⌄
Iron is more reactive than copper, so it displaces copper from copper sulphate solution: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu. The blue colour fades as copper sulphate is converted to pale green iron sulphate, and a reddish-brown coating of copper deposits on the nail. This is a displacement reaction.
A shiny brown-coloured element X on heating in air becomes black in colour. Name the element X and the black-coloured compound formed. 2 marks ⌄
X is copper (Cu). On heating in air, copper reacts with oxygen to form copper(II) oxide (CuO), which is black: 2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO.

Acids, Bases and Salts

Open this chapter →
Why should curd and sour substances not be kept in brass or copper vessels? 2 marks ⌄
Curd and sour foods contain acids, which react with the metal of the vessel to form salts that can be toxic if consumed. This is a reaction between an acid and a metal, producing a metal salt and releasing hydrogen gas in some cases.
A student dissolves solid X in water; the solution turns blue litmus red. On adding solid X to a solution of sodium carbonate, a gas evolves that turns lime water milky. Identify the gas and the general property of X being demonstrated. 3 marks ⌄
The gas is carbon dioxide (CO₂). Solid X is an acid — acids turn blue litmus red, and acids react with carbonates to release CO₂ gas: Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂. CO₂ turns lime water milky due to the formation of insoluble calcium carbonate.

Metals and Non-metals

Open this chapter →
Name a metal which reacts with cold water and one which reacts only with steam. Write the equation for the metal that reacts with cold water. 3 marks ⌄
Sodium reacts vigorously with cold water: 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂. Iron reacts only with steam (not cold water): 3Fe + 4H₂O(steam) → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂.
Why is it that non-metals do not usually displace hydrogen from dilute acids? 2 marks ⌄
Displacing hydrogen from an acid requires losing electrons to form positive ions — something metals do easily. Non-metals tend to gain electrons rather than lose them, so they generally can't push hydrogen out of an acid the way a reactive metal can.

Carbon and its Compounds

Open this chapter →
Why does carbon form largely covalent compounds rather than ionic ones? 2 marks ⌄
Carbon has 4 electrons in its outermost shell. Gaining or losing 4 electrons to form an ion would take a huge amount of energy, making ionic compound formation unfavourable. Instead, carbon shares its electrons with other atoms, forming stable covalent bonds.
An organic compound X with molecular formula C₂H₆O reacts with sodium metal to liberate hydrogen gas. Name compound X and write the reaction. 3 marks ⌄
X is ethanol (C₂H₅OH). 2CH₃CH₂OH + 2Na → 2CH₃CH₂ONa + H₂. This confirms the presence of the −OH functional group, since sodium reacts with the hydrogen in the hydroxyl group specifically.

Light — Reflection and Refraction

Open this chapter →
An object is placed at a distance of 25 cm from a concave mirror of focal length 15 cm. Find the position of the image. 3 marks ⌄
Using the mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, with u = −25 cm and f = −15 cm: 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/15 − (−1/25) = −5/75 + 3/75 = −2/75, so v = −37.5 cm. The image forms 37.5 cm in front of the mirror — real and inverted, since v is negative.
Why does the sky appear blue during the day but the sun appears reddish at sunrise and sunset? 3 marks ⌄
Blue light has a shorter wavelength than red light and is scattered far more strongly by air molecules (Rayleigh scattering), so the scattered blue light reaches our eyes from all directions, making the sky look blue. At sunrise and sunset, sunlight travels through a much greater thickness of atmosphere, scattering away most of the blue light before it reaches us — only the less-scattered red and orange wavelengths remain, giving the sun its reddish colour.

Electricity

Open this chapter →
Two resistors of 4Ω and 6Ω are connected (a) in series and (b) in parallel. Find the equivalent resistance in each case. 3 marks ⌄
(a) Series: R = R₁ + R₂ = 4 + 6 = 10Ω. (b) Parallel: 1/R = 1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12, so R = 12/5 = 2.4Ω. Notice the parallel combination gives a resistance lower than either individual resistor — that's a general property of parallel circuits.
Why is the tungsten filament of an electric bulb protected inside a glass bulb filled with an inert gas? 2 marks ⌄
Tungsten glows white-hot when current passes through it, and at that temperature it would burn if exposed to air. Filling the bulb with an inert gas (like argon or nitrogen) prevents the filament from reacting with oxygen, letting it get hot enough to glow brightly without burning out quickly.
