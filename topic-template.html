Electrostatics

How charges create fields, how those fields exert force, and how that force shapes everything from lightning to photocopiers. This page is the standard template used for all 25+ units — duplicate it to build a new topic.

Placeholder model · drag to rotate

About this topic

A point charge doesn't just sit there — it reshapes the space around it. This model shows two charges and the field lines between them, so students can see attraction, repulsion, and field density directly instead of inferring it from arrows on paper.

Learning objectives

  • Describe how like and unlike charges interact through a field
  • Relate field line density to field strength at a point
  • Predict the force on a test charge placed within the field
  • Connect this model to Coulomb's Law calculations
