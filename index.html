Why 3D

Biology has always been spatial. Now it looks like it.

A cell membrane, a DNA strand, an orbital — none of them are flat. We stopped teaching them like they were.

◐

Rotate instead of memorize

Turn a model until the structure clicks — most students stop needing the labels after a few drags.

◑

Same model, every device

Runs in the browser — no software, no headset, works on a school laptop or a phone.

◒

Built around board syllabi

Every unit is mapped to a Grade 10–12 topic, so 3D exploration stays tied to what's actually examined.
