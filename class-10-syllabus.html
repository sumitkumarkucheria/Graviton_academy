Chapter 01 Unit I · Chemical Substances

Chemical Reactions and Equations

How to write and balance a chemical equation, and the four basic patterns almost every reaction on the syllabus falls into — plus what oxidation looks like in daily life, from a rusted gate to butter going rancid.

  • Writing and balancing chemical equations
  • Combination, decomposition, and displacement reactions
  • Double displacement reactions and precipitation
  • Oxidation and reduction
  • Effects of oxidation in daily life — corrosion and rancidity

What makes something an acid or a base at the ion level, how the pH scale captures that in a single number, and the everyday salts — baking soda, washing soda, plaster of Paris — that come from acid-base chemistry.

  • Acids and bases in terms of H⁺ and OH⁻ ions
  • Reactions of acids and bases with metals and carbonates
  • The pH scale and its importance in everyday life
  • Common salts: NaOH, bleaching powder, baking soda, washing soda
  • Plaster of Paris and water of crystallisation

  • Physical and chemical properties of metals and non-metals
  • Reactions with oxygen, water, and acids
  • The reactivity series of metals
  • Formation of ionic bonds and properties of ionic compounds

Chapter 04 Unit I · Chemical Substances

Carbon and its Compounds

Carbon's ability to form long chains and rings (catenation) is what makes organic chemistry possible — this chapter covers how those compounds are named, classified, and how they react.

  • Covalent bonding and the versatile nature of carbon
  • Saturated and unsaturated hydrocarbons
  • Homologous series and nomenclature of carbon compounds
  • Chemical properties: combustion, oxidation, addition, substitution
  • Ethanol, ethanoic acid, soaps and detergents

  • Nutrition: autotrophic and heterotrophic modes
  • Respiration: aerobic and anaerobic pathways
  • Transportation of materials in plants and in humans
  • Excretion in plants and in humans

  • The nervous system and structure of a neuron
  • Voluntary, involuntary, and reflex actions
